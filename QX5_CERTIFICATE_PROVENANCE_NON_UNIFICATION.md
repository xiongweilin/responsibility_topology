## 1. Candidate A — surviving provenance structure

Preferred provisional name:

```text
pre-refinement discriminator-unavailability witness
```

Current evidence base:

```text
one mechanism-specific Web-PKI survivor only
```

The surviving structure is not the elementary aliasing fact. It is the evidence-provenance problem:

> **Before a later correct refinement is known, can independently available evidence establish that a task-required discriminator is unavailable at the relied-upon decision boundary rather than merely ignored, hidden, noisy, or miscalibrated?**

Candidate-A provenance profile:

### What defines the policy/candidate class?

The class is induced by the actually relied-upon decision boundary and the information/discriminators available there for the bounded task.

The Web-PKI case concerns selective provenance-sensitive remediation under a legacy trust representation that could not distinguish the relevant certificate provenance/compliance property at the required granularity.

### Who certifies the class boundary?

Not a hypothetical future refinement.

The boundary must be established from the actual pre-refinement system interface/decision vocabulary plus source-backed evidence about what discriminator was unavailable at that boundary.

### Where does the independent task constraint come from?

From task/accountability requirements whose evidential source is independent of the later refinement. In the Web-PKI case, the relevant pressure is selective security remediation under simultaneously recorded compatibility/interoperability constraints.

### What establishes failure?

Pre-refinement evidence that the decision boundary cannot support the task-required selective discrimination.

### What is known before refinement?

Only the negative task-relative fact:

```text
a required discriminator is unavailable at the relied-upon boundary
under the stated evidence conditions.
```

### What remains unknown?

```text
the correct future refinement;
the complete finer classification;
a generic representation-independent certificate mechanism.
```

### What conclusion is licensed?

Only:

```text
mechanism-specific pre-refinement discriminator-unavailability witness.
```

Not:

```text
generic representation inadequacy;
which distinction must be added;
revision authorization;
InsufficiencyCertificate.
```

A finite represented class `H_V`, whose boundary may come from:

```text

E2 formal model;
E3 institutional catalogue;
E4 analyst/designer enumeration.
```

### Who certifies the class boundary?

The relevant authority is the enumeration mechanism itself, and its authority is task-relative.

The active domain kill showed:

```text

E2 can establish model-relative diagnosis exhaustion already captured by incomplete-model diagnosis;
E3/E4 can lack semantic authority to define the complete task universe.
```

### Where does the independent task constraint come from?

From an independently justified test/acceptance family `C`, such as observational consistency or protocol compatibility/security requirements.

### What establishes failure?

Failure of every candidate in the represented finite class relative to `C`.

### What is known before any refinement?

At most:

```text
the current finite class has no satisfying member relative to C.
```

### What remains unknown?

Depending on the domain:

```text
whether the candidate class is the correct task universe;
what lies outside the class;
whether the larger task is actually defeated;
what alternative representation/refinement is appropriate.
```

### What conclusion is licensed?

The active domain cycle licensed only native conclusions such as:

```text

no acceptable current negotiation;
finite fault catalogue insufficient as response/model coverage.
```

It did not license a distinct QX certificate.

Candidate B is therefore eliminated, not dormant.

## 3. Superficial logical commonality

A and B can both be forced into an abstract shape such as:

```text
forall c in C_V, Fail_T(c)
```

or:

```text
no currently represented option satisfies T.
```

This commonality is rejected as a unification basis.

Why:

1. it is an elimination schema shared by many ordinary theories;
2. it erases the provenance question that made Candidate A nontrivial;
3. it erases enumeration authority, which was decisive for Candidate B;
4. it turns the intended conclusion into near-definition expansion;
5. mature neighboring theories already instantiate the same skeleton for diagnosis, negotiation, model rejection, planning, and constraint satisfaction.

Therefore:

```text
shared logical skeleton
-/->
shared QX theory.
```
