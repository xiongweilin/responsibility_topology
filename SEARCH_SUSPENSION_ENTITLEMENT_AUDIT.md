# Search-Suspension Entitlement Audit

Status: **REPOSITORY-LEVEL GOVERNANCE AUDIT / NO NEW THEORY TRACK**.

Formalization: **NO**.

Parent governance: `SEARCH_SCOPE_PROVENANCE.md` and `RESEARCH_STATE.md`.

This audit asks one question only:

> **Given the search scope actually recorded so far, which existing freeze/stop/dormancy actions are entitled, and what conclusions are they not entitled to support?**

It does not ask whether QX or QC phenomena exist in reality. It does not introduce a `SearchClosure` predicate, a new QX/QC object, or a new research ladder.

---

## 1. Freeze actions must be typed before they are justified

Use four distinct governance actions:

```text
ConstructionFreeze
CandidateFamilyClosure
EpistemicSearchSuspension
GovernanceResourcePause
```

They are not interchangeable.

### ConstructionFreeze

Meaning:

```text
current evidence does not authorize new object construction / theorem-surface expansion / Lean opening.
```

This is evidence-relative and theory-relative.

### CandidateFamilyClosure

Meaning:

```text
a preregistered or explicitly bounded candidate family has completed its allowed elimination/audit cycle.
```

This is scope-relative. It does not imply that all mechanisms in reality were searched.

### EpistemicSearchSuspension

Meaning:

```text
active search may be suspended because the recorded search process itself is sufficiently broad / saturated / low-marginal-return to justify stopping on epistemic grounds.
```

This requires independent search-scope provenance.

### GovernanceResourcePause

Meaning:

```text
active search is not currently pursued because resources/priority/governance do not authorize continued search, while the reality hypothesis remains unresolved and an external wake path remains available.
```

This does **not** require a saturation claim, because it is not an epistemic claim that the search was sufficient.

Permanent firewall:

```text
GovernanceResourcePause
!= EpistemicSearchSuspension
!= SearchSaturation
!= SearchExhaustion
!= NoSuchPhenomenon
```

## 3. Search-suspension burdens and present status

For this repository, a saturation-based `EpistemicSearchSuspension` would require at least the following burdens.

```text
S1  search question and admissible positive criterion were fixed before the relevant search;
S2  mechanism coverage is materially diverse rather than one-industry/one-mechanism sampling;
S3  more than one search strategy was used, including adversarial/absorption search;
S4  source-unavailable / unobservable cases are tracked separately from negative cases;
S5  marginal structural novelty decline is evidenced over a declared search sequence/budget;
S6  externally actionable wake conditions are preserved.
```

These burdens are governance criteria only. They are not a formal theorem schema.

### QX

```text
S1: SUBSTANTIALLY MET for completed Candidate-A/B cycles;
S2: PARTIALLY MET across heterogeneous mechanisms;
S3: MET at the current research-method level;
S4: PARTIAL / not systematically complete across all empirical search;
S5: NOT ESTABLISHED;
S6: MET through E-WAKE/T-WAKE governance.
```

Therefore:

```text
QX EpistemicSearchSuspension based on saturation: NOT EARNED
```

### QC

```text
S1: MET for the hardened positive/residual admission rule;
S2: PARTIALLY MET across several distributed/semantic/authority/currentness mechanisms;
S3: MET at the current research-method level;
S4: PARTIALLY MET through source-target/source-viability discipline;
S5: NOT ESTABLISHED;
S6: MET only as source-viable/event-driven re-entry, not as proof of search sufficiency.
```

Therefore:

```text
QC EpistemicSearchSuspension based on saturation: NOT EARNED
```

The decisive missing burden in both tracks is not raw case count. It is the absence of a defensible, recorded marginal-novelty argument over a declared search scope/budget, together with incomplete mechanism/source coverage.

## 5. Why the current governance pause is still allowed

Failure to earn saturation-based search suspension does **not** create an obligation to continue active search indefinitely.

The repository may legitimately remain dormant when all of the following are explicit:

```text
TheoryGate remains closed under current evidence;
no currently licensed construction step exists;
active search is not represented as exhaustive or saturated;
known blind spots remain recorded;
RealityHypothesis remains UNRESOLVED;
new naturally encountered evidence can enter through an explicit wake/admission path;
no negative-reality claim is inferred from inactivity.
```

This licenses only:

```text
GovernanceResourcePause
```

not:

```text
SearchComplete
SearchSaturated
NoPositiveCaseExists
NoSuchPhenomenon
```

In short:

```text
permission not to spend more search effort now
!=
evidence that more search would be epistemically unproductive.
```

